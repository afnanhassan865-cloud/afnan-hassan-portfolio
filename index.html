02 / SELECTED WORK

How I think through
customer situations.

These are training-based scenarios created to demonstrate my support approach. They contain no confidential client information.

01
ORDER ACCURACY

Wrong item received

Situation

A customer received a different product from the one ordered.

My approach

Acknowledge the error, confirm the order details and request the minimum evidence needed. Offer the approved replacement or refund path, document the case and set a clear follow-up time.

Quality outcome

The customer knows exactly what happens next, while the internal team receives a complete and actionable handoff.

02
SHIPPING FOLLOW-UP

Delivery has stopped moving

Situation

Tracking has not updated and the customer is worried the order is lost.

My approach

Check the latest available tracking facts, avoid unsupported promises, explain the next escalation step and commit to a specific update window.

Quality outcome

A calm, factual reply protects trust and prevents duplicate contacts.

03
REFUNDS & BILLING

Refund status request

Situation

A return was accepted, but the customer cannot see the refund yet.

My approach

Verify the recorded refund status and payment method, explain the expected processing window, add a clear internal note and escalate only when the stated window has passed.

Quality outcome

The reply is empathetic, policy-aligned and easy for another agent to continue.
